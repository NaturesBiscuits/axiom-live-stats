Local DOM reads approximately every 5s, including new coins. Charts fit available observations and break across missing readings or delays over 7.5s. Window changes require the full recorded duration and a valid baseline within ±2.5s of the window start; they end at the latest valid observation. Coverage is valid occupied 5s slots / expected slots between the first and last retained attempts in this currency. USD liquidity changes are observed valuations, not confirmed additions/removals or a safe-to-buy guarantee. DOM readability does not verify Axiom's upstream freshness. Separate IndexedDB retention: 24 hours / 10,000 actual attempts globally, saved in 15s batches. After retention removes the beginning, summaries start at the first retained observation.
